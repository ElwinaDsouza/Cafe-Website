Our Story & Sourcing

Conscious Coffee Culture

Founded with a passion for slow coffee and minimal aesthetics, Cafe Walnut was designed as a sanctuary from the urban rush.A quiet corner. A warm mug. A simple idea.We started in 2021 with an old espresso machine, a few wooden chairs, and a love for slow mornings. We wanted to create a space where time slows down.


We source 100% of our beans directly from shade-grown, organic micro-lots in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Every roast is carefully tuned to highlight the bean's natural terroir.
